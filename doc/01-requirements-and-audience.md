# Task 01: Requirements Analysis & Target Audience Strategy

## 1. Project Overview & Business Objectives
- **Brand Name**: Atelier Lumière Melbourne
- **Category**: Luxury Bespoke Nail Salon & Nail Health Care
- **Location**: 128 Collins Street, Melbourne VIC 3000, Australia
- **Core Market**: Australia (Melbourne CBD & inner suburbs: South Yarra, Carlton, Toorak, Fitzroy, Docklands)
- **Target Demographics**: Discerning individuals aged 18–55 who value natural nail longevity, minimalist aesthetics, meticulous hygiene, and an unhurried sanctuary experience.
- **Primary Conversion Metric**: Frictionless appointment bookings via the website (`POST /api/bookings`).

---

## 3. Ethical Conversion Optimization (Zero Dark Patterns)
In strict accordance with modern UX best practices:
- **No False Scarcity**: Time slots reflect real scheduling blocks without fake timers.
- **No Manipulation**: Pricing is 100% transparent in AUD with no surprise surcharges.
- **No Misleading Claims**: Customer quotes and rating metrics are marked as authentic sample data representative of real salon treatments.
- **Friction Reduction**: Cognitive load is minimized by breaking the booking sequence into 6 digestible steps rather than an intimidating single-page mega-form.

---

## 4. Australian Market Nuances
- **Tone of Voice**: Understated, confident, welcoming, and warm. Australian beauty consumers favor authenticity, natural results, and high hygiene standards over aggressive marketing puffery.
- **Pricing & Currency**: AUD pricing clearly shown with inclusions (cuticle refinement, hand massage, and consultations included).
- **Communication Channels**: Prominent integration with Australian phone dialer links (`tel:+61391234567`), Melbourne CBD location, and Google Maps directions.
