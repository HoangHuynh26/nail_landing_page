# Task 03: Information Architecture & Section Hierarchy

## 1. Structural Breakdown
The landing page layout guides the user through 10 logically ordered sections:

1. **Announcement Bar**: Concise location & operating hours (128 Collins St, Melbourne • Mon–Sat 9:30 AM – 7:00 PM) with a direct link to Google Maps.
2. **Sticky Translucent Navbar**:
   - Monogram emblem (`AL`) + "Atelier Lumière Melbourne".
   - Navigation links: Services, Pricing, Standards, About, Gallery, Reviews, FAQ.
   - Segmented Language Switch `[ VI | EN ]`.
   - Primary CTA button "Book Appointment".
   - Mobile: Sliding sheet drawer with full touch targets.
3. **Hero Section**:
   - Editorial split composition: Left narrative with dual CTAs, verified Google review rating badge; Right high-res editorial photography of almond-shaped nails on travertine stone with floating "BIAB Japanese Gel" tag.
4. **Standards & Trust Bar**: 4-card matrix communicating non-toxic formulas, hospital-grade autoclave sterilization, master artists, and quiet sanctuary.
5. **Curated Services**: Tabbed category filters (All Treatments, Manicure & BIAB, Spa Pedicure, Minimalist Art) with duration, AUD pricing, and direct "Select Service" triggers.
6. **Transparent Pricing Table**: Structured matrix with clear inclusions note.
7. **Why Choose Us**: 4 pillar cards highlighting Japanese & European e-file techniques, single-use sterilized pouches, Australian botanical infusions, and the 7-Day Atelier Guarantee.
8. **About The Atelier**: Warm narrative on salon craftsmanship, tranquility, and natural beauty with interior atelier photography.
9. **Editorial Asymmetrical Gallery**: Visual proof of real BIAB, Glazed Pearl Chrome, Micro-French, and spa pedicures with direct booking triggers.
10. **Client Reviews & Testimonials**: Authentic feedback from Melbourne suburbs (South Yarra, Docklands, Carlton) with 5-star ratings and verified labels.
11. **Interactive FAQ**: Accordion addressing BIAB care, walk-ins, cancellations, and nearby Collins St parking.
12. **Final Conversion CTA**: Calming invitation to book with reassurance badges (No prepayment, 24h cancellation, sterilized tools).
13. **Footer**: Rich footer with Australian business numbers (ABN), opening hours, location, tel links, Instagram, Facebook, and dual language switch.
